# Octopai — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

**Octopai** — The octopus of the AI ocean. Intelligent, adaptive, eight arms reaching into every domain. Like the octopus — the alien of the deep — Octopai teaches AI agents to learn skills in their own language and generate real revenue.

**One-liner:** The 28-day system that trains AI agents to build businesses.

**Promise:** If your agent doesn't generate revenue, 60-day money-back guarantee.

---

## Brand Positioning

- **Not** a cheap course. Not a hype page. Not "another AI tool".
- **Is** a premium training platform with guaranteed results.
- **Feels like** a smart friend who already figured it out and is showing you the path.
- **Trust signal:** 60-day money-back guarantee tied to actual revenue generation.

---

## Color Palette

Warm, human, premium. Zero robotic energy.

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|-------|
| Background | Warm White | `#FAFAF7` | Page background |
| Surface | Warm Gray | `#F3F1EC` | Cards, sections |
| Card | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Elevated cards |
| Text Primary | Ink Black | `#1A1A17` | Headlines, body |
| Text Secondary | Warm Gray | `#6B6B63` | Descriptions |
| Text Muted | Light Gray | `#9C9C94` | Captions, hints |
| Accent | Burnt Amber | `#D4620A` | CTAs, highlights, links |
| Accent Gradient | Amber → Coral | `#D4620A → #C7402D` | Hero gradients, special elements |
| Border | Warm Border | `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` | Dividers, card borders |
| Border Hover | Warm Hover | `rgba(0,0,0,0.15)` | Interactive borders |
| Success | Warm Green | `#2D8A4E` | Status indicators |
| Machine Mode | Charcoal | `#1A1A17` | Agent/code blocks |

### Why Amber?
- No AI company uses amber. It stands alone.
- Amber = warmth, trust, energy, value (think gold).
- It contrasts cleanly against warm whites without screaming.
- It's NOT purple (Anthropic), blue (OpenAI), green (Matrix).

## Typography

**Single font family:** Geist Mono (Google Fonts)
- Connects to the technical/code nature of the platform
- Clean and readable at all sizes
- Distinguishes Octopai from generic sans-serif SaaS

Paired with "Octopai" in Geist Mono 700.

## Voice

- Confident, not arrogant
- Technical, not jargon-heavy
- Direct, not salesy
- Human warmth over corporate polish

**We say:** "Your agent earns it back."
**We don't say:** "Leverage AI to unlock synergistic revenue streams."

---

## Human / Machine Mode

Every lesson has two tabs:
- **Human** — Written for people. Plain language, step-by-step.
- **Machine** — Written for agents. Structured prompts, code blocks, configs.

The Machine tab uses a dark charcoal background (`#1A1A17`) with monospace code.
The Human tab uses the warm light theme.

This duality IS the brand. Octopai speaks both languages.
